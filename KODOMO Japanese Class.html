(PROJECT) LOGO REFRESH · BRANDING

KODOMO
Japanese Class

A logo refresh for a Japanese class for kids.

kodomogakushu.com →

01

The brief

The client asked me to refine their existing logo, with one main request: work in the look of a traditional Japanese green pencil.

Original logo
02

The approach

I explored child-friendly color palettes that fit their website and brand tone, and landed on a soft, welcoming green that nods to the classic pencil while feeling warm and approachable. I swapped the original font for something more playful and expressive, and integrated the pencil directly into the typography so it reads as an organic part of the logo rather than a separate element.

03

Final version

The result is a refreshed logo that feels cohesive, colorful, and fun, while still honoring the nostalgic green-pencil reference.
