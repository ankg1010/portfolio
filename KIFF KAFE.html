KIFF KAFE

Branding for Kiff Kafe, a café in Los Angeles.

12229 West Pico Boulevard, Los Angeles, CA 90064  ·  kiffkafe.com →

01

Logo redesign

Kiff Kafe originally had a logo inspired by the Nazar Boncuğu, the Turkish evil-eye charm. I redesigned it into a simpler, more minimalist mark.

02

A coffee
guide

I illustrated a guide that explains each type of coffee on the menu.

03

Social

For their Instagram, I took a photo of an actual dish and shaped it into a heart.

04

Brand
applications

The mark and Kiffkafe red carried across coffee bags, cups, mugs, tumblers, and merch.
